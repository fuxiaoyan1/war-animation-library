# 十大阵法战役资料与地图来源 / Top Formation-Battle Sources and Map Leads

本文件是“十大战役阵法动画”长期计划的资料底座。它优先记录一手或近一手叙事、可靠二手综述、地图/战场位置候选和动画化不确定性。具体动画开工时，每一役还应拆成独立 `docs/sources/<battle>.md`。

This file is the research base for the long-running "top ten formation battles" animation program. It prioritizes primary or near-primary narrative sources, reputable secondary summaries, map/battlefield leads, and animation uncertainty notes. When production starts, each battle should still receive its own `docs/sources/<battle>.md` file.

## 选入标准 / Selection Criteria

- 阵列和兵种部署可还原：能看出中军、两翼、骑兵、步兵、弓弩/远射、战象/炮兵等位置。
  Formation recoverability: sources must support center, wings, cavalry, infantry, missiles, elephants/artillery, or equivalent positions.
- 战斗阶段可拆分：开局部署、接敌、局部突破、包围/崩溃、追击等阶段有叙事依据。
  Phase modeling: deployment, contact, breakthrough, envelopment/collapse, and pursuit should be source-backed.
- 地图可制作：战场位置、地形约束、道路/河流/村镇或至少战术平面构图可找到依据。
  Map feasibility: the battlefield, terrain constraints, roads/rivers/settlements, or at least tactical-plan geometry must be supportable.
- 不把现代示意图当作事实本身。现代地图只能作为构图参考，必须回到古典/史书/官方/学术来源核对。
  Modern diagrams are composition references, not facts; claims must be checked against classical, historical, official, or scholarly sources.

## 1. Waterloo / 滑铁卢

### Primary and Institutional Sources / 一手与机构来源

- National Army Museum, `Battle of Waterloo`: <https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/battle-waterloo>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Waterloo Battlefield`: <https://www.britannica.com/place/Waterloo-Battlefield>
- Wikimedia Commons map category, `Maps of the Battle of Waterloo`: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Maps_of_the_Battle_of_Waterloo>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Wellington's army holds the Mount St Jean ridge; Hougoumont, La Haye Sainte, and Papelotte are forward strongpoints.
  威灵顿主阵依托圣让山脊，乌古蒙、拉海圣和帕佩洛特是前出支撑点。
- French attacks require distinct phases: Hougoumont diversion, d'Erlon infantry attack, Allied heavy cavalry countercharge, Ney's cavalry charges against squares, La Haye Sainte crisis, Imperial Guard final attack, Prussian pressure at Plancenoit/right flank.
  法军攻势必须拆阶段：乌古蒙牵制、德尔隆步兵进攻、盟军重骑反击、内伊骑兵冲击方阵、拉海圣危机、近卫军最后进攻、普军在普朗斯努瓦/右翼施压。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Map confidence is high because battlefield villages, ridge line, farms, roads, and later memorial geography are well documented.
  战场村镇、山脊、农庄、道路和后世纪念地保存较完整，地图可信度高。
- The animation should use modern terrain only as a base and redraw tactical layers; modern monuments must not be presented as 1815 terrain.
  动画可用现代地形作底，但战术层需重绘；现代纪念物不能当作 1815 年战场要素。

## 2. Cannae / 坎尼

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- Polybius, `Histories`, Book 3: <https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/3*.html>
- Livy, `Ab Urbe Condita`, Book 22, Perseus Digital Library: <https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Liv.+22.44>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of Cannae`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Cannae>
- World History Encyclopedia, `Battle of Cannae`: <https://www.worldhistory.org/Battle_of_Cannae/>
- Wikimedia Commons map category, `Maps of the Battle of Cannae`: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Maps_of_the_Battle_of_Cannae>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Roman infantry mass advances into Hannibal's deliberately flexible center.
  罗马重步兵密集推进，压入汉尼拔有意设置的弹性中军。
- Carthaginian cavalry superiority clears the wings; African infantry turns inward; the Roman mass becomes enclosed.
  迦太基骑兵清理两翼，非洲重步兵内折，罗马步兵集团被合围。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- The battlefield is tied to the Ofanto/Aufidus river plain, but exact tactical orientation differs by reconstruction.
  战场与奥凡托/奥菲杜斯河平原相关，但具体朝向在不同复原中有差异。
- Use Commons diagrams only as phase-layout references; final route geometry should be rebuilt from source logic.
  Commons 示意图只作阶段构图参考；最终路线几何应按史料逻辑重建。

## 3. Gaugamela / 高加米拉

### Primary, Scholarly, and Reference Sources / 一手、学术与参考来源

- Arrian narrative excerpt, Iran Chamber Society: <https://www.iranchamber.com/history/achaemenids/arrian_battle_of_gaugamela.php>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of Gaugamela`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Gaugamela>
- World History Encyclopedia, `Battle of Gaugamela`: <https://www.worldhistory.org/Battle_of_Gaugamela/>
- Cambridge Core, `The Battle of Gaugamela and the Question of Visibility on the Battlefield`: <https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/iraq/article/battle-of-gaugamela-and-the-question-of-visibility-on-the-battlefield/F32530FFEA360D4CE959361638C7CFC2>
- Wikimedia Commons map category, `Maps of the Battle of Gaugamela`: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Maps_of_the_Battle_of_Gaugamela>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Macedonian line should show the phalanx center, Companion cavalry on Alexander's right, a second/reserve line against envelopment, and light infantry reacting to chariots.
  马其顿线应显示方阵中军、亚历山大右翼伙伴骑兵、防包抄的第二/预备线，以及应对战车的轻装步兵。
- Persian line should show Darius in the center, broad cavalry wings, prepared plain, and scythed chariots.
  波斯线应显示大流士中军、宽大骑兵两翼、预处理平原和镰刀战车。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Exact battlefield location remains debated; do not present one candidate as certain.
  战场精确位置仍有争议，不应把某一候选地点表现为确定事实。
- First animation pass can use a tactical abstract plain with named geographic uncertainty rather than high-precision terrain.
  第一版动画可使用带不确定性注记的战术平面，而不是宣称高精度地形复原。

## 4. Pharsalus / 法萨卢斯

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- Caesar, `Civil War`, Book 3, Perseus Digital Library: <https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Caes.+Civ.+3>
- World History Encyclopedia, `The Battle of Pharsalus`: <https://www.worldhistory.org/article/697/battle-of-pharsalus/>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of Pharsalus`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Pharsalus>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Caesar and Pompey both deploy Roman lines, but Caesar holds a hidden/withheld fourth line to counter Pompey's cavalry.
  凯撒和庞培都以罗马线列展开，但凯撒保留第四线以反制庞培骑兵。
- The animation must show the cavalry flank action before the infantry decision; otherwise the battle loses its tactical meaning.
  动画必须先表现骑兵侧翼战再表现步兵决胜，否则会丢失战术核心。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Battlefield location is debated around Pharsalus/Palaepharsalus and the Enipeus plain.
  战场位置围绕法萨卢斯/旧法萨卢斯和埃尼佩乌斯平原存在争议。
- Use a documented uncertainty overlay and avoid precise archaeological claims in the first pass.
  第一版应加不确定性图层，避免精确考古式断言。

## 5. Raphia / 拉菲亚

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- Polybius, `Histories`, Book 5: <https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/5*.html>
- Livius, `Battle of Raphia`: <https://www.livius.org/articles/battle/raphia-217-bce/>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- This is a strong Hellenistic deployment case: phalanx center, elephants forward/on wings, cavalry wings, light troops between heavy components.
  这是典型希腊化大阵案例：方阵中军、战象前置/布于两翼、骑兵两翼、轻装部队填充重兵之间。
- It is useful for building reusable phalanx and elephant-rendering components before Hydaspes.
  它适合先沉淀方阵和战象渲染组件，再复用到海达斯佩斯。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- The battle is generally associated with the Raphia/Rafah area, but tactical micro-terrain should be treated as schematic.
  战役通常关联拉菲亚/拉法一带，但战术微地形应按示意处理。

## 6. Hydaspes / 海达斯佩斯

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- World History Encyclopedia, `Battle of Hydaspes`: <https://www.worldhistory.org/article/660/battle-of-hydaspes/>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of the Hydaspes`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-the-Hydaspes>
- Livius, `Hydaspes`: <https://www.livius.org/articles/battle/hydaspes-326-bce/>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Model the operation in two layers: river crossing/deception, then battlefield formation against Porus.
  作战要分两层：渡河欺敌，随后对波罗斯大阵的战场部署。
- Porus' elephant line is the visual center; Macedonian cavalry and phalanx pressure must be timed around elephant disruption.
  波罗斯战象线是视觉中心；马其顿骑兵和方阵压力必须围绕战象扰乱来编排。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- The Hydaspes is the Jhelum River; exact crossing and battle sites are debated.
  海达斯佩斯即杰赫勒姆河；具体渡河点和战场位置有争议。
- First pass should use river geometry and relative positions rather than a fixed archaeological point.
  第一版应使用河流几何和相对位置，而不是固定考古点。

## 7. Leuctra / 留克特拉

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- Xenophon, `Hellenica`, Book 6, Perseus Digital Library: <https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.+Hell.+6.4>
- World History Encyclopedia, `Battle of Leuctra`: <https://www.worldhistory.org/Battle_of_Leuctra/>
- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of Leuctra`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Leuctra>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- The core animation problem is asymmetric depth: Theban left wing deep column against Spartan right, with a refused/held-back weaker side.
  动画核心是非对称纵深：底比斯左翼深阵冲击斯巴达右翼，弱侧拒止或后置。
- This battle is ideal for explaining weighted attack and oblique pressure with minimal map complexity.
  该战适合用较低地图复杂度解释集中重心和斜向压力。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Battlefield terrain can be simplified around Leuctra/Boetia; formation geometry matters more than precise village detail.
  可围绕留克特拉/维奥蒂亚简化地形；阵形几何比村镇细节更重要。

## 8. Agincourt / 阿金库尔

### Institutional and Reference Sources / 机构与参考来源

- Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Battle of Agincourt`: <https://www.britannica.com/event/Battle-of-Agincourt>
- Historical Association, `The archer's stake and the battle of Agincourt`: <https://www.history.org.uk/publications/resource/8654/the-archers-stake-and-the-battle-of-agincourt>
- Musee de l'Armee, `Battle of Agincourt`: <https://www.musee-armee.fr/ExpoChevaliersBombardes/battle-agincourt.html>
- Battlefields Trust hub: <https://www.battlefieldstrust.com/resource-centre/battleview.asp>
- Wikimedia Commons map category, `Maps of the Battle of Agincourt`: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Maps_of_the_Battle_of_Agincourt>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- English dismounted men-at-arms hold the center; longbowmen deploy on the sides with stakes; French men-at-arms attack through a constrained, muddy front.
  英军下马重装步兵居中，长弓手带木桩在两侧展开；法军重装兵在泥泞狭窄正面推进。
- The animation must show terrain compression and congestion, not only arrow fire.
  动画必须表现地形压缩和拥塞，而不能只表现箭雨。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Agincourt has useful institutional and heritage references, but the exact tactical layout and stake arrangement remain debated.
  阿金库尔有较多机构/遗产资料，但具体战术布置和木桩方式仍有争议。

## 9. Jingxing / 井陉

### Primary Sources / 一手来源

- Sima Qian, `Records of the Grand Historian`, `Biography of the Marquis of Huaiyin`, Chinese Text Project: <https://ctext.org/shiji/huai-yin-hou-lie-zhuan/zh>
- Sima Guang, `Zizhi Tongjian`, Chinese Text Project candidate volume: <https://ctext.org/zizhi-tongjian/11/zh>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- Han Xin's back-to-water deployment, feigned weakness, Zhao pursuit, and camp-flag seizure are the core.
  韩信背水列阵、示弱诱敌、赵军追出、奇兵夺营易帜是核心。
- The battle is less about symmetric array detail and more about formation psychology plus camp control.
  该战不是对称阵列细节，而是阵法心理和营地控制。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Use Jingxing/pass geography, river/road constraints, and camp-relative geometry; do not overclaim exact coordinates.
  使用井陉关隘、河流/道路约束和营地相对几何，不要过度宣称精确坐标。
- OpenStreetMap may help identify modern pass/river/settlement reference points: <https://www.openstreetmap.org/>
  OpenStreetMap 可辅助核对现代关隘、河流、居民点参考。

## 10. Fei River / 淝水

### Primary and Reference Sources / 一手与参考来源

- `Zizhi Tongjian`, volume 100, Wikisource: <https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E8%B3%87%E6%B2%BB%E9%80%9A%E9%91%91/%E5%8D%B7100>
- `Zizhi Tongjian`, volume 100, Chinese Text Project: <https://ctext.org/zizhi-tongjian/100/zh>

### Formation Notes / 阵法要点

- The animation should show river-line standoff, request/inducement to withdraw, Qin movement becoming panic, Zhu Xu's shout, and collapse across/near the river line.
  动画应表现临河对峙、请退诱动、秦军移动转为恐慌、朱序呼喊、河线附近崩溃。
- It is a formation-breakdown battle, not a detailed weapon-by-weapon array battle.
  它是阵列崩解战，不是兵器细分阵列战。

### Map Leads and Uncertainty / 地图线索与不确定性

- Use Shouyang/Shouchun, Fei River, Huai River, and surrounding route constraints as the initial map frame.
  初版地图以寿阳/寿春、淝水、淮河及周边路线约束为框架。
- River course and exact tactical positions need specialist review before final animation.
  河道变迁和精确战术位置需要专题复核后再进入最终动画。

## Production Rules for This Series / 本系列制作规则

- Each animation gets a dedicated source file before implementation.
  每部动画开工前都要有独立来源文件。
- Every map claim must be labeled as `certain`, `probable`, `schematic`, or `contested`.
  每个地图判断必须标注为 `确定`、`较可能`、`示意` 或 `有争议`。
- Primary-source claims and modern-map reconstructions must be separated in data comments and story text.
  一手资料叙述与现代地图复原要在数据注释和故事文本中分开。
- No animation should say "historically exact formation" unless it is explicitly backed by source language and scholarly consensus.
  除非史料文字和学术共识都明确支持，否则动画不得声称“精确复原历史阵法”。
